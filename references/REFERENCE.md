## Approved visual facts

- The overall design is symmetric, geometric and approximately 48 inches square including the outside pieced checkerboard.
- **Four** corner blocks, **four** side blocks, **one** central star block.
- **Four same-size** blue floral strips plus **four separately pieced blue floral squares** at the ends of two of those strips.
- **The black-and-cream checkerboard, including the narrow outer border, is pieced from solid fabrics.** Do not shop for checkerboard-print yardage.

## Fidelity and to-do

This initial commit documents the image names and builds a diagrammatic construction reference. The original source PNG files came from the chat attachments, rather than from a git path. The diagram is not a perfect tracing of the fabric print or patchwork geometry. Preserve the original files as source-of-truth references when you add image assets to this repo. In particular, don't derive exact triangle cutting instructions solely from the schematic.
