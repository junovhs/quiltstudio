Pieced border tally

The black-and-cream outer checkerboard is made from individual fabric pieces, not a printed checkerboard.

380 squares total at a provisional ½″ finished square size: 190 cream, 190 black. Each is cut 1″ square with ¼″ seams. Two side borders use 94 squares each, and the top/bottom use 96 each.

This assumes a single row of alternating squares around the outside. The black/cream corner continuity and best strip-piecing method remain to be decided.

Backing, batting & binding (separate from top budget)

Backing: approx. 3¼ yd of regular 42–44″ fabric, pieced, or 1⅔ yd of 108″ wide fabric.
Batting: at least 56″ × 56″.
Binding: approximately ½ yard if made from separate fabric.

Backing and batting estimates allow extra area for quilting; actual requirements depend on your longarm / domestic-quilting setup.

Blue floral reservation

Four matching strips, cut 4¼″ × 40″, plus four floral corner squares, cut 4¼″ × 4¼″. Reserve the remaining floral fabric for the star, bands, and triangle accents.

No separate checkerboard-print fabric is being purchased. Both the interior checkerboards and the outer border use the cream and black yardage.
