# Project 001 — Floral medallion quilt

**Status:** Design-planning stage (not ready for batch cutting)  
**Last captured:** 2026-11-14  
**Finished top target:** 48 × 48 inches, **including** the narrow outer checkerboard border  
**Seam allowance:** standard ¼ inch, provisional  
**Units:** inches unless marked otherwise  
**Primary artwork:** the user's six images listed in [references/REFERENCE.md](references/REFERENCE.md).

## Design intent, established in conversation

- Symmetrical square medallion quilt in royal-blue floral fabric, chocolate brown, warm cream, and black.
- Center: brown eight-point star surrounding a black/cream grid, with blue floral space/accent triangles.
- Four matching square **corner blocks** with cream/brown/black/floral geometric motifs.
- Four rectangular **side blocks** with brown/cream mirrored triangles and black/cream checkerboard panels; two appear vertically rotated.
- Four blue floral *main strips are exactly equal length*; the two long horizontal border assemblies are formed by adding **one separately cut floral square to each end** of two of those strips. No special longer main strips.
- Outer border is **pieced from cream and black fabric**, not purchased checkerboard-print fabric. Interior checkerboards are also pieced from those two fabrics.
- Finished size of the entire top including checkerboard = 48 × 48 inches.

## Dimension proof

- Central 3 × 3 block grid: **9½ + 20½ + 9½ = 39½** inches in either direction.
- With blue floral sides: **39½ + 3¾ + 3¾ = 47** inches.
- Each top/bottom assembled blue floral border: **3¾ + 39½ + 3¾ = 47** inches; two identical main strips plus four separate corner squares total.
- With ½″ finished checkerboard strip on all four edges: **47 + ½ + ½ = 48** inches.

### Directional placement of four blue floral strips

1. Attach two plain 39½″-finished floral strips to the left and right edges of the 39½″ center.
2. On each of the other two identical 39½″ floral strips, sew a 3¾″-finished floral square at each end.
3. Attach those two assembled 47″ strips across the top and bottom.
4. The blue-floral-framed top finishes 47″ square, before the black/cream border.

## Pieced outer checkerboard calculation (provisional)

To obtain a single row of squares **½″ finished**, cut **1″ × 1″** squares with ¼″ seams. This is a small, potentially fiddly unit.

If side border strips are sewn first, before top and bottom:
- Two **47″ side lengths:** 94 squares per side = 188.
- Two **48″ top/bottom lengths:** 96 squares per side = 192.
- **Total: 380 alternating squares**, or **190 cream and 190 black** under a balanced two-color sequence.

This **conditional** estimate assumes a one-square-wide checkerboard border and no miter, and may need correction for the exact corner sequence and border construction. The original picture suggests a very narrow checker effect but no final square size has been approved. Sewing individual 1″ raw squares in extremely long rows may be awkward; test alternate strip-piecing and border scale before cutting 380 squares.

## Fabric budget (quilt top only)

Assumes 42–44″ wide quilting cotton, at least 40″ usable width:

| Fabric | Provisional buy amount | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Royal blue floral | **2 yd** | Outer floral bands/strips, corner squares, negative space and accents |
| Warm cream | **1 yd** | Triangles, interior grids, pieced outer checker border |
| Chocolate brown | **1 yd** | Central star and corner/side triangles |
| Black | **½ yd** | Interior grids and pieced outer checker border |
| Checkerboard **print** | **0 yd** | NOT USED — border is pieced |

**Total proposed top purchase: 4½ yd** (generous estimate; not a finalized yield calculation). This corrects the earlier estimate that included ⅓ yd of printed checkerboard fabric, which the user explicitly rejected.

The four blue strips require four 4¼″-wide cross-width cuts at 40″ length (approximately 17″ of fabric length before squaring/trimming) plus four 4¼″ squares. Check usable width and print direction before cutting.

### Separate backing and finishing budget

- Pieced backing: roughly **3¼ yd** of regular-width 42–44″ quilting cotton, **or** **1⅔ yd** of 108″ wide backing fabric.
- Batting: at least **56 × 56** inches.
- Separate binding fabric: **½ yd** estimate, if used.
- These estimates presume the backing/batting needs a working margin beyond the 48″ top. Verify with quilting method and actual fabric width.
